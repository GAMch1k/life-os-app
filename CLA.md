## In short

This summary is here to help you read the agreement; it is not part of it. The agreement below is what counts.

- **You keep your copyright.** You give the maintainer a license to your contributions, not ownership of them, and you can still use your own work in any way you like.
- **The maintainer may license your contributions in other ways too:** under the AGPL-3.0-only, under other open-source licenses, and under commercial or proprietary licenses, for example to sell licenses or to run a paid hosted Life OS.
- **Your contributions stay open source.** Everything accepted into Life OS also stays available to everyone under the AGPL-3.0-only (or a later open-source license) in the public repository.
- **You confirm the work is yours to give:** you wrote it, your employer agrees if they have a say in it, and you tell us about any third-party code or restrictions you know of. You don't owe anyone support or a warranty.
- **To sign,** post a comment on your pull request, from your own GitHub account, with exactly this sentence:

  ```
  I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA
  ```

  You sign once, and it covers your later contributions too. The `CLA` check then records, publicly on the repository's `cla-signatures` branch, your GitHub login and account ID, this version, a link to your comment, the pull request, and the time. Each Life OS repository keeps its own record, so you sign once in each repository you contribute to.
