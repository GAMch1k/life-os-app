# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience
- Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all of Life OS's community spaces: the repositories [GAMch1k/life-os](https://github.com/GAMch1k/life-os) and [GAMch1k/life-os-app](https://github.com/GAMch1k/life-os-app), their issues, pull requests, discussions, and reviews. It also applies when someone officially represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer, [@GAMch1k](https://github.com/GAMch1k) on GitHub: mention them on the issue or pull request concerned. If the report should not be public, say there only that you need to reach the maintainer privately, without details, and the maintainer will find a private way to continue. You can also use GitHub's **Report content**, which goes to GitHub.

Every report is reviewed promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy is respected.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that don't follow this Code of Conduct, and explains moderation decisions when appropriate. Depending on how serious the behavior is, the consequence is one of:

1. **Correction**: a private, written warning that explains what was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning**: a warning with consequences for continued behavior, and no interaction with the people involved for a while.
3. **Temporary ban**: no interaction or public communication with the community for a while.
4. **Permanent ban**: no public interaction with the community at all.
